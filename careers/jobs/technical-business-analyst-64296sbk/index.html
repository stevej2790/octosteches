Technical Business Analyst

Apply To This Job

About the Role

We’re looking for a Technical Business Analyst who can bridge the gap between business needs and technology solutions. This is a hybrid role, ideal for someone who’s analytically minded, comfortable with technology, and eager to grow into a more senior position over the next few years.

You’ll work closely with experienced team members to learn the business, understand client requirements, translate them into actionable tasks for developers, and perform light testing to ensure quality deliverables. This is not a coding role—instead, you’ll focus on understanding logic, systems, and processes, helping connect the dots between business and technical teams.

This position offers an excellent learning path: you’ll be mentored by senior team members who will retire in a couple of years, providing you with deep industry knowledge and hands-on experience to take on greater responsibility in the future.

Key Responsibilities

  • In a mid-term, collaborate with clients and internal stakeholders to gather, clarify, and document business requirements.

  • Translate business needs into logical, technical specifications for the development team.

  • Work with project management tools (e.g., Azure DevOps) to create, assign, and track tasks.

  • Participate in team meetings to understand project status and upcoming deliverables.

  • Support light project management activities, ensuring timelines and deliverables are aligned.

  • Perform functional and user acceptance testing (UAT) on reports, small applications, and data-driven solutions.

  • Validate outputs and ensure consistency between requirements and final deliverables.

  • Learn about the company’s specific industry, processes, and systems to become an internal expert over time.

Required Skills & Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Engineering, or a related field.

  • 3–4 years of professional experience, ideally in business analysis, data analysis, or a similar technical coordination role.

  • Strong analytical and logical thinking skills.

  • Solid understanding of technology concepts (applications, databases, integrations, cloud environments).

  • Experience with Azure DevOps or other project management tools is a plus.

  • Excellent communication skills—able to understand client needs and clearly convey them to technical teams.

  • Comfortable working in a structured, process-oriented environment with mentorship and ongoing learning.

  • Detail-oriented, organized, and proactive in problem-solving.

  • Experience with testing or QA activities is a plus (functional, UAT, or data validation).

Nice to Have

  • Exposure to reporting or analytics environments.

  • Familiarity with Agile methodologies.

  • Experience working with cross-functional technical teams.

  • Understanding of Azure or cloud-based platforms.

Why Join Us

  • Opportunity to learn directly from senior experts and grow into a key contributor.

  • Exposure to a mix of business analysis, technical coordination, and light project management.

  • Supportive and collaborative environment where training and mentorship are part of the culture.

  • The chance to become a long-term specialist in a niche industry with strong career potential.
