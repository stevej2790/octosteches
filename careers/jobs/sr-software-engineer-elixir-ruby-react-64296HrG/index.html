Role Overview

We are seeking a Senior Software Engineer to join our team and contribute to the development of scalable, high-performance applications and platform initiatives. The ideal candidate is passionate about building production-ready systems, collaborating across teams, and driving technical excellence through modern software engineering practices.

Key Responsibilities

  • Develop high-quality, performant, and testable code that supports company-wide initiatives.
  • Build and maintain applications using modern technologies such as Elixir, Ruby, React, TypeScript, GraphQL, Kafka, AWS Lambda, PostgreSQL, and DynamoDB.
  • Contribute to technical decision-making and help shape the future architecture of software systems.
  • Continuously learn and adopt new technologies, frameworks, and engineering practices.
  • Review pull requests and provide constructive feedback to team members.
  • Collaborate closely with other engineers through pair programming and team-based development.
  • Deploy user-facing applications and features to production environments.
  • Partner with Product and Engineering Management to deliver business value and improve platform capabilities.
